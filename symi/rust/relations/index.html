                                                                                            Relations and predicates

                                                                                            Relations construct symbolic formulas. They are distinct from host-language truth queries.

                                                                                            Relations#

                                                                                            Relation nodes are used as equations for solve/solveset, as piecewise conditions, and as boolean-valued results.
