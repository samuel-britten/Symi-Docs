Reduces the antisymmetric cross products of a cylinder pair to their exact Wronskian value.

A determinant built from a cylinder fundamental pair is zero-free for a reason no cancellation can reach: J(nu,z) Y(nu+1,z) - J(nu+1,z) Y(nu,z) = -2/(pi z) is a fact about the functions, not about their arguments. Substituting it is what turns “the algebra cannot decide this determinant” into “this determinant is nonzero everywhere on the chart”, and it does so at every order — integer orders included, where the two functions of the first kind would already have stopped being independent of each other.

The input is expected to have passed through reduce_contiguous_orders first, so that the only orders left are the two the identity is stated at.
