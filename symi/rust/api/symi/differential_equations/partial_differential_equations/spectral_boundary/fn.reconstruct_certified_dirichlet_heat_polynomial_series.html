Builds the certified sine-series solution for the exact Dirichlet heat datum x(pi-x).

This deliberately narrow route is the first infinite-modal family. It records the shared summation expression and only accepts the classical interval [0, pi], the positive sine family, and the polynomial datum for which the Fourier expansion theorem is exact. The one-mode governing identity is replayed before the series is admitted; endpoint and initial expansion evidence are established by the fixed Dirichlet sine theorem rather than by treating an unevaluated summation as a verified boundary value.
