1// Real algebraic numbers. A real embedding on top of the
2// abstract number fields (algebraic_numbers/) and the ℚ-only real-root
3// isolation (polynomial/real_roots.rs): a real_algebraic_number is a squarefree
4// defining polynomial over ℚ together with an isolating interval that brackets
5// exactly one of its real roots. The module adds the operations the two
6// substrates lacked — a total order, field arithmetic, the sign of a polynomial
7// at a real algebraic point, and real-root isolation over a real algebraic
8// extension (the cylindrical-algebraic-decomposition lifting primitive) — each
9// decided exactly, with the numeric evaluator used only as a test guard.
