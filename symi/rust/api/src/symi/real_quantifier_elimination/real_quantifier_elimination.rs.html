1// Real quantifier elimination and semialgebraic applications.
2//
3// The application tier on the CAD cluster. It turns the
4// sign-invariant decomposition and the efficiency projection operators into user-facing
5// decision power: effective real quantifier elimination by solution-formula
6// construction (Hong 1992, sign-vector → quantifier-free DNF), with Collins–Hong
7// (1991) partial CAD pruning the lift the moment a cell's truth is settled, and two
8// consumers — nonlinear constraint / inequality solving over ℝⁿ and global symbolic
9// optimization on semialgebraic sets.
10//
11// The module is a pure consumer of the core: a quantified formula is a small Rust
12// `tarski_formula` whose quantifier-free matrix reuses the logic module's connectives
13// and the
14// relation builtins, and a QE result is a quantifier-free `expression_reference` built
15// through the logic layer. It adds no new ExpressionKind and no new CAD data type; it
16// reads the CAD cell arena, drives `lift_over_cell` through
17// `select_projection_operator`, and decides every verdict by the real-algebraic-number
18// layer's exact sign / order.
